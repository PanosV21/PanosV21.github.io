Panos Vorgias

PhD Candidate in Economics · Boston University

My research lies at the intersection of innovation, intellectual property, industrial organization, and public policy, with a particular interest in how institutions shape firms' innovation decisions and the diffusion of new technologies.

My job market paper studies how changes in the allocation of intellectual property rights over federally funded inventions affected the direction of private innovation. Exploiting a major change in U.S. government patent policy in the early 1980s, I show that stronger contractor ownership redirected innovative activity toward projects with higher private value but lower technological spillovers.

I am on the 2026–27 economics job market and expect to graduate in spring 2027.
