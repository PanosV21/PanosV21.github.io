Panos Vorgias

PhD Candidate in Economics · Boston University

My research lies at the intersection of innovation, intellectual property, industrial organization, and public policy, with a particular interest in how institutions shape firms’ innovation decisions and the diffusion of new technologies.

My job market paper identifies a tradeoff in granting firms ownership of publicly funded inventions: higher private value, but weaker knowledge diffusion. Exploiting a major change in U.S. government patent policy in the early 1980s, I show that stronger contractor ownership redirected innovative activity toward projects with higher private value but lower technological spillovers. The nature of this tradeoff varies with firms’ research capabilities.
